<Update label="October 2026" description="SDK 4.0">
  - `acme@4` adds `users.all()` for auto-pagination and retries failed requests twice by default.
  - Requires Node.js 20 or later.
</Update>

<Update label="September 2026" description="Orders API">
  - `GET /v1/orders` accepts a `status` filter.
  - Order totals are now returned as `totalCents`.
</Update>
